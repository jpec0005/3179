How is sleep affected by a persons lifestyle and what implications does it have for their wellbeing

This visualisation aims to highlight what age groups and gender sleep patterns are through weekdays, weekends and their average duration. Secondly it sheds light on lifestyle choices such as employment, diet and smoking status to see if poor wellbeing choices affects sleep. Lastly how accommodation risk of homelessness affects how well people sleep.


Average Sleep Hours and Duration

This section goes over the onset and waking time of sexes and age groups, as well as sleep duration.

* Please note that hovering over bars/dots, that the minutes are in decimal hours besides graphs that display time in hours *



For both sexes weekend nights have a later onset time and people wake up later on weekends. As well as females having a higher average duration.



For age groups onset time for 18-24 age group for both weeknight and weekend night is more varied between 12am and 1am. compared to 55-64 where onset time sits around 11pm. Duration for groups 25-34, 35-44 and 45-54 are similar. This may be influenced by a couple of lifestyle reasons in the next section.

SHS Clients and why they sleep rough

This section goes over the Specialist Homelessness Services (SHS). A government-funded organisation in Australia that provide support, accommodation, and resources to people experiencing homelessness or at risk of losing their housing. The reason this is a focus is because their clients are deemed as "Rough Sleepers". They seek help as their accommodation varies and their vulnerabilities that may contribute to poor sleep and over all wellbeing.
